2. What the app does

ZYNOVA AI lets you chat with an AI, work with documents and images, use voice, and keep personal notes and memories. It offers two kinds of AI:

  • Offline AI – a Gemma model that you download once and that runs on your phone. It works without internet and has no usage limit.
  • Online AI – Google's Gemini service. It needs internet and your permission, and it is limited to 5 answers (tokens) per day. When the tokens for the day are used, the app switches to Offline AI automatically, and the tokens return the next day.

The app is provided free of charge. We may change, limit or remove features, including the daily Online AI limit, at any time.
